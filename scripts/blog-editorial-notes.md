# USK Galle journal: editorial sources and follow-up

Prepared 15 October 2026. New articles use author `System` / `system`. Publication dates are the writing date, not backdated event dates.

## Sources used

- `src/app/data/events.js`: event dates, meeting point, time, descriptions, collaborators, upcoming state and recap-photo counts.
- `public/gallery-images/`: existing event photographs reused as covers; no Instagram images were downloaded.
- `src/app/data/artworks.js` and `artists.js`: credited work linked to Meet #3, #4 and #5, artist names and profile routes.
- Public Instagram profile: https://www.instagram.com/urbansketchersgalle/ — profile identity and website link were visible. Individual posts required sign-in; their captions were **not** used as evidence.

The articles cite the relevant internal recaps and artist pages. The Instagram link is for community updates, not a claim that inaccessible captions were verified. Public search also surfaced organiser announcements elsewhere, but these articles do not rely on extra details from those snippets.

## Scope

- History of the available community archive.
- December 2025 sketch walk and completed numbered Meets #1–#5.
- A clearly dated upcoming preview for Meet #6, not a retrospective.
- No invented attendance counts, participant quotes, weather, exact routes or founder names.
- Existing unrelated articles, their bylines and their dates are unchanged.

## Details to ask the organiser later

1. When was USK Galle formally started, by whom, and how did the December 2025 walk lead to it? Is the community an officially recognised chapter, and if so from what date?
2. For each meet: confirmed route/stops, workshop leaders, collaborators, attendance if recorded, and one or two memorable moments or participant quotes with attribution/permission.
3. What are the original Instagram post links/captions for the walk and each meet? Add them to the relevant article after checking them.
4. Who photographed the event collections, and how should they be credited? Current captions identify the community archive rather than inventing an individual photographer.
5. Has Meet #6 acquired a confirmed date or venue? Update its dated preview when announced, and write the recap only after it occurs.

These are follow-up questions, not blockers to the factual archive-based articles. Prefer adding confirmed personal details later over making the existing copy sound like an eyewitness account.
